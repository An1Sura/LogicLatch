The path looks clear. Why is it stopping?

A stopped box and a blocked photoeye do not tell you which component failed. LogicLatch connects the signals to help you choose the next useful check.

OBSERVED SIGNALS → WORKING HYPOTHESIS

Motor commanded on + high current + product not clearing the photoeye. Investigate mechanical resistance before replacing the sensor. A drive fault or bad current reading still needs to be ruled out.

  1. Compare the cluesA photoeye issue could explain “blocked,” but not by itself the elevated motor current. Safety_OK is reported healthy; that signal is not permission to access the mechanism.
  2. Inspect safely beyond the visible boxFor real equipment, stop and isolate hazardous energy using the site procedure. Qualified maintenance should check roller drag, belt tracking and drive condition against the equipment instructions. Do not reach into a running conveyor.
  3. Test a hypothesis—not an alarm numberLook for a roller that resists rotation or other evidence of binding. The existing registers cannot distinguish a bearing problem from belt or drive resistance.
DEMO INSPECTION FINDING

One roller bearing binds under load. The conveyor looks unobstructed, but extra drag prevents the box from clearing the photoeye. The highlighted bearing is an authored demo finding—not measured bearing telemetry.

Next action: qualified maintenance should service or replace the affected roller/bearing per the equipment instructions, then verify free movement and alignment before following the site restart procedure. Clearing the box or resetting the alarm alone leaves the cause.

Use the evidence to narrow the search; verify the cause before changing parts.

FAULT 601 / GUIDED INVESTIGATION

Clearing the box may not fix the cause.

A guide can shift inward and pinch each new box. The photoeye then stays blocked because product cannot pass—not necessarily because the sensor failed.

DEMO CAUSE · NOT SENSOR-CONFIRMED

Misaligned side guide at the discharge. Current and blockage support a mechanical restriction, but cannot identify its exact location.

  1. Make the cell safeFor real equipment, stop and isolate hazardous energy using the site procedure before reaching in or adjusting a guide.
  2. Distinguish sensor trouble from dragA blocked input alone could be a photoeye issue. Here, the running command and 9.5 A current also indicate load. Check for a pinched carton, guide rub marks or a binding roller.
  3. Check the guide—not just the boxCompare the guide gap and alignment with the approved setup. In this demo the discharge guide has drifted inward; simply removing the box leaves the restriction.
  4. Correct, then prove recoveryRestore the approved guide position and secure its mounting. Follow the site restart procedure. Verify clear photoeye, normal running current and no recurring timeout.

The amber guide is the scenario’s assumed cause. The backend has no guide-position sensor, so this highlight is an illustration—not a measured diagnosis.
